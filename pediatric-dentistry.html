Care, By Situation

Find the path that matches where you are right now.

Your child's first dental visit

Best scheduled around their first birthday, or within six months of the first tooth. The goal isn't a full cleaning — it's building familiarity, checking early development, and giving you guidance on brushing, teething and habits before small issues start.

No pressure, no drama — if your child isn't ready to sit in the chair on day one, that's normal, and we adjust.

Routine exams, cleanings & prevention

Twice-yearly visits combine a gentle cleaning, fluoride when appropriate, and a growth check. Sealants and digital X-rays are used selectively — only when they'll actually change how we care for your child, and always explained first.

Cavities & dental problems

If treatment is needed, we explain what we found, show you images where useful, and walk through options — including tooth-colored restorations — before scheduling anything. You'll always understand the "why" before you say yes.

Urgent & emergency appointments

Toothaches, chipped teeth and dental injuries don't wait for a convenient time — call us and we'll work to see your child the same day. Tell us what happened and we'll let you know exactly what to do until your visit.

Before You Book

The questions most parents are actually asking.

What if my child is scared or has trouble cooperating?

We slow down. Every step is introduced before it happens, and we take the time a nervous child needs — including short "warm-up" visits with no treatment at all, if that's what helps.

How long does an appointment usually take?

A routine exam and cleaning is typically 30–45 minutes. First visits often run shorter, since the focus is comfort, not a full cleaning.

Do you treat teenagers too?

Yes — we see patients from their first tooth through the teenage years, adjusting our approach as they grow.

Will I be pressured into treatment my child doesn't need?

No. Every recommendation is explained in plain terms, with the reasoning behind it, before it's ever scheduled. If something can wait, we'll tell you that too.
