The approach

Diagnose first. Prioritize second. Create third.

Not "how many articles should we publish" — but "what are the right customers searching for, where is the site failing to meet that demand, and what deserves priority." Everything else — what to write, improve, consolidate or drop — follows from that answer.

  1. Demand — real search demand and the intent behind it, not just keyword volume.
  2. Coverage — an honest read of what the site already answers.
  3. Gaps — where the site and competitors currently under-serve real demand.
  4. Priority — ranked by opportunity and effort, not treated as equally urgent.
  5. Architecture — what to create, improve, consolidate or reposition, and how it connects.
  6. Measurement — what's actually moving, checked against the plan.
The objection this usually raises

"Can't AI or a freelance writer just do this?"

A writer or an AI tool can produce pages quickly once someone has decided what to write. Neither one decides which pages your specific business should prioritize, or why — that decision depends on your market, your competitors, and where the commercial opportunity actually sits. That's the part that happens before a single word gets written, and it's the part this service is built around.

Before you ask

The honest answers, so the next step isn't a leap of faith.

How quickly will I rank?

There's no honest answer that guarantees a timeline or a ranking position — anyone who promises one is guessing. What's realistic is a prioritized plan, clear reasoning behind it, and measurement over time so you can see what's actually moving.

Will more traffic actually turn into customers?

Not automatically — and that's the point of prioritizing by relevance rather than volume. The goal is visibility among people searching for problems you can genuinely solve, not the largest traffic number possible.

I've already tried SEO and it didn't work.

That's worth understanding specifically, not assuming. An initial look at what was done, what it targeted, and what happened afterward is part of the diagnosis — not something to guess at in advance.

How much does this cost?

Pricing depends on the scope of the assessment and strategy work involved, and hasn't been set here. That's part of the first conversation, not a number to guess at on this page.
