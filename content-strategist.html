Why this isn't "just SEO"

Content production and content strategy solve different problems.

A writer or an AI tool can produce pages quickly. Neither one decides which pages your specific business should prioritize, or why. That decision requires understanding your market, your competitors, and where the commercial opportunity actually sits — the part that happens before a single word gets written.

Before you ask

The honest answers to the questions people actually have.

Can't I just do this myself?

Yes — if you have the time to research real search demand, audit your existing content honestly, and prioritize against competitors objectively. The value here is mostly in that diagnosis and prioritization, and in the outside perspective that catches what's hard to see from inside your own business.

Why not just hire a writer?

A writer produces the pages. This decides which pages are worth producing, in what order, and why — before anyone starts writing. Most wasted content budget comes from skipping that step, not from bad writing.

Can't AI just create the content?

AI is genuinely useful for producing drafts. It doesn't know which topics your specific market searches for, what your competitors already cover well, or what deserves priority for your business. That's the strategic decision this service focuses on.

How quickly will I rank?

There's no honest, responsible answer that guarantees a timeline or a ranking position — anyone who promises one is guessing. What's realistic is a prioritized plan, clear reasoning behind it, and measurement over time so you can see what's actually moving.

Will more traffic actually turn into customers?

Not automatically — and that's the point of prioritizing by relevance rather than volume. The goal is visibility among people searching for problems you can genuinely solve, not the largest traffic number possible.

I've already tried SEO and it didn't work.

That's worth understanding specifically, not assuming. An initial look at what was done, what it targeted, and what happened afterward is part of the diagnosis — not something to guess at in advance.

How much does this cost?

Pricing depends on the scope of the assessment and strategy work involved, and hasn't been set here. That's part of the first conversation, not a number to guess at on this page.
