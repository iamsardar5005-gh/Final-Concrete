The method

Six decisions, made in order, that turn content into a system.

Not twelve deliverables bolted together — one line of reasoning, each step earning the next, producing a single working system for your business: audience understanding, search-intent analysis, opportunity research, positioning, pillars, and a connected journey to action.

  1. 01

    Discover

    What are your customers actually searching, asking, and needing? Real demand and intent, not assumptions about what they want to hear.

  2. 02

    Prioritize

    Which of those opportunities matter most to this business right now — weighed by commercial relevance, competitive gap, and effort.

  3. 03

    Position

    What should you become known for? A clear point of view and topic territory, not a scattershot list of subjects.

  4. 04

    Create

    What content should exist, and what job does each piece do — content pillars, priority topics, and the site journey that holds them.

  5. 05

    Connect

    How does discovery become trust, and trust become action? The conversion-path thinking that links pages into one journey.

  6. 06

    Measure

    What signals prove the strategy is working — and a 90-day roadmap so the plan keeps moving after this engagement ends.

Why a strategist, specifically

A writer produces pages. AI drafts fast. Neither one decides what's worth doing.

A freelance writer is excellent once you already know what to write and why. AI is excellent at producing drafts once the topic is chosen. Neither replaces the judgment that comes first — what should exist, for whom, and what it's supposed to accomplish. That judgment is the actual bottleneck, not the writing.

Is this SEO, content marketing, or conversion strategy?
All three, treated as one decision instead of three separate hires — a visitor doesn't experience your site in departments.
I already have a website — why isn't it working?
Most underperforming sites aren't broken. They're unfocused: built without a clear read on intent, priority, or a path to action.
Do I just need more traffic?
No. Traffic that can't become a qualified inquiry isn't the goal — the right visitors are.
