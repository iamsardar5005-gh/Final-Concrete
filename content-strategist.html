The method

Six decisions, made in order, that turn content into a system.

Not twelve deliverables bolted together, and not a bucket labeled “SEO” or “content marketing” on its own — one line of reasoning that covers search visibility, content, and conversion together, because a visitor never experiences your site in departments. Each step earns the next, producing a single working system: audience understanding, search-intent analysis, opportunity research, positioning, content pillars, and a connected journey to action.

  1. 01

    Discover

    What are your customers actually searching, asking, and needing? Real demand and intent, not assumptions about what they want to hear.

  2. 02

    Prioritize

    Which of those opportunities matter most to this business right now — weighed by commercial relevance, competitive gap, and effort. This is how you know what's worth creating, before anything gets written.

  3. 03

    Position

    What should you become known for? A clear point of view and topic territory, not a scattershot list of subjects.

  4. 04

    Create

    What content should exist, and what job does each piece do — content pillars, priority topics, and the site journey that holds them.

  5. 05

    Connect

    How does discovery become trust, and trust become action? The conversion-path thinking that links pages into one journey.

  6. 06

    Measure

    What signals prove the strategy is working — tracked against a 90-day roadmap, so you can see the plan moving, not just publishing happening.
